Zanshin privacy policy

Notes and on-device recovery copies

Zanshin stores notes on your device and does not send them to external servers or translation services. There is no cloud sync. The on-device recovery copy helps restore notes on the same device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes the notes stored on the device.

If you use iOS device backups such as iCloud Backup, iOS may include app data in those backups.

Accounts, ads, and tracking

No account is required. Zanshin collects no identifiers or account information and uses no ads, analytics, or tracking. The app uses Capacitor for on-device features.

Contact

The support page links to GitHub Issues. Posts are public. Keep your notes and personal information private.
