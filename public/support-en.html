Zanshin support

Write, read, and search

Choose New note to start writing. The title is optional, and changes save automatically on this device. Open a note to read it and choose Vertical or Horizontal. Search looks through titles and note text. Choose Pin to top to put a note in the top group.

To change the interface language, open About this app and choose Language. You can follow system settings or choose Japanese or English.

Saving, recovery, and deletion

If saving fails, follow the message on screen to try again, or use Copy text and Copy title to keep the original. Viewing a recovery version leaves saved data as it was. If the display fails, you can copy any unsaved text shown before you reload.

Undo is available for the last deletion for 10 seconds, until you close or restart the app. Removing a protected recovery version is permanent.

Contact

GitHub Issues posts are public. Keep your notes and personal information private. If helpful, include the app version, display language, device and OS, what you did, and any message you saw.
